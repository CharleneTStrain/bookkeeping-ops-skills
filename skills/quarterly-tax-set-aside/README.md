# Quarterly tax set-aside

**The problem:** Nobody takes taxes out of a freelancer's pay, the quarterly due dates don't match the calendar, and the first tax return often brings a bill nobody saw coming.

**What it does:** Starts with one number: your next due date and the safe payment for it, worked out from last year's tax return using the IRS's own rule for avoiding the underpayment penalty, so you can schedule it today. Then builds a set-aside rate on your profit (self-employment tax, your federal bracket and your state's rate) so the April bill is covered too. Handles married households where a spouse's paycheck withholding can do the work, seasonal businesses, and businesses that buy inventory. If you already owe or missed payments, it walks through what to do in order: file on time, pay what you can, payment plan options and penalty relief questions. It never estimates this year's tax bill or a penalty amount; those go to your CPA.

**Use it with:** last year's tax return and roughly what you've earned so far this year.

**You get:** the next due date and what to pay, federal and state, plus an Excel workbook with your safe payments, a set-aside log, a payment tracker, options if you owe, and CPA questions. A planning tool, not tax advice.
